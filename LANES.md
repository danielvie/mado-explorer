## Scheduling and promotion policy

Use continuous supervisor-driven per-lane scheduling, not rounds or group-completion barriers. Each lane follows Implement -> Review -> supervisor approval -> serial integration -> integration validation -> next task from updated main. A passing lane queues immediately without waiting for other lanes. Its next commission starts only after its own integration and validation succeed. Use individual native Agent completion notifications for future stages; the saved rolling-feature-wave runner is bounded batch preparation, not the continuing scheduler. Admit queued implementation or fresh rework into five shared writer slots, giving concrete fixes priority; admit fresh exact-head reviews into three reviewer slots. Process receipts promptly and overlap independent writing/reviews with the separate integrator. A review gates its candidate and dependent slices, not unrelated implementation. At three ready candidates awaiting integration, prioritize integration over new dispatch; blocked candidates do not occupy ready capacity. Never invent work or bypass a decision/safety gate. Freeze each candidate head, baseline, owned paths and check evidence before review. Persist handoffs in tracked lane-local documents or root handoff/, never only in target/. Reconcile live ownership and durable heads after interruption before relaunch. Repeated supervisor provider failures halt new dispatch; workers may finish their bounded commissions but never approve or integrate themselves.
